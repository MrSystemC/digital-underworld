# Human Continuity Archive — Concept Charter

**Companion concept for GENESIS ARCHIVE #001 · 2 November 2026**

## Purpose

The Human Continuity Archive is a proposed public-interest knowledge initiative intended to make high-quality, verifiable resources easier to discover across domains that influence long-term human resilience. It is a proposal, not an operating institution. No foundation, endowment, grant program, research network or formal governance body is represented as existing.

## Initial knowledge domains

1. Public health and health-system resilience.
2. Food security, agriculture and water access.
3. Clean energy, energy storage and grid resilience.
4. Climate adaptation, biodiversity and ecosystem restoration.
5. AI safety, trustworthy computing and responsible technology.
6. Education, scientific literacy and cultural preservation.
7. Disaster preparedness, humanitarian coordination and conflict prevention.
8. Space science and planetary-risk awareness.

## Operating principles

- Evidence first: distinguish peer-reviewed evidence, official guidance, expert consensus, open questions and opinion.
- Open access: prefer lawful, openly licensed resources and state reuse restrictions.
- Interoperability: use durable identifiers, machine-readable metadata and documented APIs.
- Pluralism: include credible regional, linguistic and disciplinary perspectives.
- Safety and privacy: do not publish personal, confidential or dangerous operational information.
- Measurable outcomes: publish milestones, coverage, corrections and usage metrics.
- No pay-to-influence: token ownership must not buy control over scientific conclusions or editorial findings.

## Proposed 12-month pilot after an actual project launch

- Months 0–2: legal host or incorporation, governance and editorial policy.
- Months 2–4: public catalogue covering three pilot domains.
- Months 4–6: independent expert review and multilingual pilot.
- Months 6–9: documented API, export format and partner integrations.
- Months 9–12: transparent impact report and next-year funding decision.

These are proposed milestones, not existing achievements or commitments. The schedule begins only after an operator, governance and funding are established.

## Relationship to the NFT

GENESIS ARCHIVE #001 is a symbolic digital artwork associated with this concept. The token does not grant ownership of the proposed archive, its data, a legal entity, treasury, intellectual property, investment returns or governance rights. Any future benefits require separate public, legally reviewed terms adopted by a real operator.
