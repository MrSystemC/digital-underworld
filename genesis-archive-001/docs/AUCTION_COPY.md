# Auction and Press Copy

## English

### GENESIS ARCHIVE #001 — The Human Continuity NFT

**One-of-one AI-assisted digital artwork · Ethereum ERC-721 · Token ID 1 (planned)**

An hourglass becomes a vessel for two possible futures: Earth held as a living memory above, and a regenerative civilization imagined below. Encircling the form are emblems of the fields on which human continuity depends — health, science, knowledge, clean energy, ecology, cooperation, justice and exploration.

GENESIS ARCHIVE #001 is conceived as the visual frontispiece to the proposed Human Continuity Archive, an initiative intended to make reliable knowledge and practical resources more discoverable across the systems that shape our shared future. The artwork is both a collectible and a statement of intent: preservation is not passive storage, but the transfer of knowledge into better decisions.

The work is AI-assisted and is intended as a single token within its dedicated ERC-721 contract. Metadata is intended for IPFS, with the token URI fixed at deployment. The contract signals a 5% secondary-sale royalty under EIP-2981; actual payment depends on marketplace implementation.

**Collector note:** the token conveys ownership of the blockchain token only. It does not convey copyright, commercial reproduction rights, a share in a fund or organization, investment returns or guaranteed future utility. The associated archive remains a concept-stage proposal.

**Estimate / reserve:** not set. Determine only after independent specialist appraisal, provenance review, rights review and auction-house acceptance. No guaranteed valuation is represented.
